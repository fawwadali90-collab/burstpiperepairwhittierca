Every copper repair starts with an assessment of the whole run, not just the leak. One pinhole in otherwise clean copper is a simple section repair. Pinholes in several spots, or pipe walls thinned along the run, mean the corrosion is systemic and patching one hole just moves the next failure down the line. FAQ

Copper Pipe Repair: Common Questions

What causes pinhole leaks in copper pipes?

Internal pitting corrosion, driven by water chemistry and flow patterns, eats tiny pits through the pipe wall over years. High water pressure and aggressive water accelerate it. Once pinholes start appearing, more usually follow.

Can a pinhole leak be patched, or does the pipe need replacing?

A single pinhole in healthy pipe can be repaired with a proper coupling. Multiple pinholes along a run signal systemic corrosion, and repiping that section costs less over time than chasing leaks one by one.

How long does a copper pipe repair take?

Most single-leak copper repairs take one to two hours including opening and closing the wall. The plumbing work itself is often the fastest part.
