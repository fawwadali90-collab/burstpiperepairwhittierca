FAQ

Water Line Replacement: Common Questions

When should a water line be replaced instead of repaired?

When breaks recur, when internal corrosion has choked the line down to low pressure, or when the pipe material is at the end of its service life. Repeated repairs on a failing line cost more over time than one replacement.

What is trenchless water line replacement?

A method that installs the new pipe by bursting or pulling it through the old line's path, needing only small entry and exit pits instead of an open trench across the yard.

How long does a water line replacement take?

Most residential service line replacements finish in one to two days, depending on length, depth, soil conditions, and the method used.
