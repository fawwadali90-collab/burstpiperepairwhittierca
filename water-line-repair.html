Quick Answer

Water line repair in Whittier fixes breaks in the main service line between the meter and your house, spotted by soggy yards, low pressure, or a running meter. Trenchless and targeted-dig methods keep your landscaping intact. Call (562) 539-0483.

Repair starts with locating the break: the line is traced from meter to house, and the failure point is found with listening equipment or careful test holes. Depending on the pipe's overall condition, the fix is a section repair at the break or a recommendation to replace the run. Trenchless options can replace pipe with minimal digging when the situation fits.

FAQ

Water Line Repair: Common Questions

How do I know my water service line is broken?

Soggy or sunken areas in the yard, water pooling near the foundation, a sudden drop in whole-house water pressure, or a water meter that runs with everything off all point to a service line break.

Will you have to dig up my whole yard?

No. The break is located first, so digging is limited to the repair area. Trenchless methods can often replace the line with only small entry and exit pits.

Who is responsible for the water service line?

In most Whittier homes, the homeowner owns the service line from the water meter to the house. The water utility handles everything up to and including the meter.
