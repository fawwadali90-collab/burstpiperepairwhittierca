FAQ

Burst Pipe Leak Detection: Common Questions

How do I know I have a hidden pipe leak?

Watch for unexplained damp spots, warm areas on the floor, the sound of running water when nothing is on, a water meter that moves with all fixtures off, or a water bill that jumps without explanation. Any of these deserves professional detection.

Can you find a leak without tearing up my walls?

Yes. Acoustic and thermal detection locate the leak first, so the wall opening is small and exactly where it needs to be. Blind demolition to hunt for a leak is what happens without proper detection gear.

How long does leak detection take?

Most residential detection visits take one to two hours. Slab and underground leaks can take longer because the search area is bigger and the signals are fainter.

What happens after you find the leak?

You get the location, the likely cause, and repair options with a plain explanation. Most detected leaks move straight to repair in the same visit or a scheduled follow-up.
