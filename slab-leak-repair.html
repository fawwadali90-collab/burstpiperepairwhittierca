Quick Answer

Slab leak repair in Whittier addresses water line leaks under your concrete foundation, spotted by warm floors, damp carpet, or a spinning water meter. Detection pinpoints the leak so the fix is surgical: a small slab opening, not a demolished floor. Call (562) 539-0483.

Slab leak work starts with confirmation and location: meter testing proves the leak, acoustic gear and thermal imaging find it. Then comes the repair decision: open the slab at the exact point for a direct repair, or reroute the line overhead through walls and attic, abandoning the under-slab run entirely. Rerouting often costs less in total once floor restoration is factored in. You get both options explained plainly.

FAQ

Slab Leak Repair: Common Questions

What are the signs of a slab leak?

Warm spots on the floor, damp or moldy carpet, the sound of running water with fixtures off, a water meter that moves when nothing runs, and unexplained spikes in the water bill.

Do you have to break up my whole floor?

No. Detection pinpoints the leak first, so concrete removal is limited to a small area. In many cases, rerouting the line overhead avoids slab work entirely.

Can a slab leak be fixed without detection?

It should not be. Opening concrete without knowing the leak's location is expensive guesswork. Professional detection is what makes slab repair surgical instead of destructive.
