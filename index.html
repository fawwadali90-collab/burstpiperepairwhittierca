Know the signs

PIPE TROUBLE? ACT FAST.

Burst pipes rarely fail without warning. Catch these signs early and you turn an emergency into a scheduled repair.

01

Water Where It Should Not Be

Damp drywall, bubbling paint, or puddles with no source. Water travels, so the stain is rarely directly under the break.

02

Pressure Drop

Faucets that used to blast now trickle. A sudden whole-house pressure drop often means a supply line has split open.

03

Strange Sounds

Banging, hissing behind walls, or water running when everything is off. Pressurized leaks are loud if you listen.

04

Bill Spike

A water bill that jumps with no change in usage. The meter does not lie: water is escaping somewhere.

Seeing any of these? Do not wait for the break to get bigger.
